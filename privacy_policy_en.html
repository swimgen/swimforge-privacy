1. Summary

Swimforge builds swim workouts on your phone. The core of the app works offline and your training data stays on your device. There is no user account, no Swimforge server and no cloud sync.

The app relies on three Google services:

Service Purpose Legal basis
Google AdMob Serving ads in the free version Consent (personalised ads) / legitimate interest (non-personalised ads)
Google Play Billing Managing the premium subscription Performance of a contract
Firebase Crashlytics & Google Analytics for Firebase Crash reporting and usage measurement Consent

We do not sell your data and we do not share it with data brokers.

2. Data controller

The data controller within the meaning of the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR, EU 2016/679) is:

Swimforge, publisher of the Swimforge application.
Contact: swimgen7@gmail.com

Please use the address above for any question about this policy or to exercise your rights.

3. Data processed on your device

The following data is stored locally, in a database internal to the app and in Android system preferences. It does not leave your phone unless you deliberately choose to share it (see §6, "Data sharing").

3.1 Swimmer profile

  • First name or nickname you enter
  • Age and sex
  • Swim level (Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced, Elite)
  • Main goal and secondary goals
  • Target competition date, if any
  • Weekly training frequency
  • Pool length and unit of measurement
  • Available equipment (paddles, fins, pull buoy, kickboard, snorkel)
  • Critical Swim Speed (CSS), overall and per stroke

3.2 Training activity

  • Generated and recorded workouts (date, volume, strokes, intensity zones, target paces)
  • Answers to the five-question post-workout feedback form
  • Training programmes started and progress within them
  • Trophies, challenges, weekly streak, skill tree
  • Successive versions of your personalised adaptation model

3.3 Technical preferences

  • Language selected in the app
  • Advertising consent choices stored by the Google consent platform

3.4 What the app does not collect

Swimforge does not request or record your location, contacts, photos, microphone, camera, health sensor data, address book, or any account identifier (there is no sign-up and no login).

5. Advertising and consent

The free version of Swimforge displays ads served by Google AdMob.

On first launch, and in territories where the law requires it (European Economic Area, United Kingdom, Switzerland), the app shows a consent form managed by the Google User Messaging Platform (UMP). There you choose whether to accept or decline personalised advertising.

  • If you accept: Google may use your device's advertising identifier and technical information (device model, operating system, language, approximate IP address) to select personalised ads.
  • If you decline: ads are still shown, but in a non-personalised form. Google then processes a reduced set of data, limited to what is needed to serve and bill the ads.

You can change this choice at any time from Profile → Manage my advertising consent.

Google acts as an independent controller for this data. Its practices are described here:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses data from partner sites and apps
  • AdMob information

You can also reset or delete your advertising identifier in Android settings (Settings → Google → Ads).

6. Premium subscription

The premium subscription (monthly or yearly, with a trial period) removes advertising. It is sold and managed exclusively by Google Play.

Swimforge does not see, store or process any payment data: no card number, no cardholder name, no billing address. The app only receives the subscription state from Google Play (active / inactive, auto-renewing) and a technical purchase token.

Managing, cancelling and refunding the subscription is done in Google Play.

7. Crash reporting and usage measurement

Swimforge includes Firebase Crashlytics and Google Analytics for Firebase.

Collection is disabled by default and is enabled only after you have given consent through the form described in §5. When collection is enabled, the following may be sent to Google:

  • Technical crash reports: stack trace, device model, Android version, app version, time of the crash, anonymous installation identifier
  • Aggregated usage events: app opens, screens viewed, session length

This information is used to fix defects and understand overall usage. It contains neither your name nor the content of your workouts.

8. Data sharing

Your training data is not passed to anyone, with two exceptions, both triggered by you:

  1. Sharing a trophy. If you tap the share button, the app renders an image and hands it to the Android share sheet. You choose the receiving app. Swimforge sends nothing on its own.
  2. The Google services described in §5, §6 and §7.

No data is sold, rented or transferred to data brokers or to third-party advertisers directly.

9. Retention

  • Local data: kept for as long as the app is installed. It is erased immediately and permanently if you uninstall the app, clear its data from Android settings, or use Profile → Reset my profile.
  • Data processed by Google (advertising, crashes, usage measurement, subscription): for the retention periods set by Google in its own privacy policy.

10. Your rights

Given the architecture of the app, you exercise most of these rights directly on your device:

12. Security

Data remains inside the Android application sandbox, isolated from other apps by the operating system. Network exchanges with Google services are encrypted over HTTPS. As no method of protection is absolute, we cannot guarantee complete security.

13. Children

Swimforge is not intended for and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data about a child under 13. If you believe this has happened, write to the address in §2 and the data will be deleted.

14. Changes to this policy

This policy may change, in particular when features are added. The date of the last update appears at the top of this document. If a change is substantial, a notice will be shown in the app or in the release notes on Google Play.
